I spent 2 hours every week categorizing expenses in a spreadsheet.

Amount. Merchant. Date. Category. Payment method. Every single time.

Then I built a bot. Now it takes 5 seconds.

## The Problem

I tried YNAB. I tried expense apps. But opening another app to log "Zara €20" created friction.

I'd skip days. Data got inconsistent. The system died.

I needed something simpler. Something I was already using all day.

## The Insight

Telegram. I already checked it constantly. News, communities, messages.

What if I could type an expense there and AI would categorize it automatically?

Not simple to build. But simple to use. That's what mattered.

## The Solution

I built a bot. It needed a database. A server. AI integration to categorize.

The UX is the payoff.

Now: I type "Zara 20 euro" → the bot confirms → expense is sorted.

## The Automation

Every Monday I get a report. Top 5 expenses from last week.

Every 1st of the month I get a breakdown. Full month summary.

One input. Everything else is automated.

## What I Learned

Is it perfect? No.

Is it more consistent than YNAB? Yes.

Why? Friction kills systems. Remove friction, people actually use them.

Building the bot taught me more about my spending patterns than any fancy app ever did.

## The Real Point

The technology is secondary. The behavior is primary.

You can have the best tool in the world. If it requires friction, it dies.

I'm curious: What expense tracking system actually stuck for you?
